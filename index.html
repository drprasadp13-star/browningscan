How to take the photo

  1. Put four cubes (about 1 cm) in a clear dish on plain white paper. The paper is the colour reference.
  2. Hold the phone flat, about 30 cm above, so the dish fills a third of the frame.
  3. Keep lamp reflections and the phone's shadow off the cubes. No grey card is needed; room light, daylight or an LED lamp all work. Avoid washed-out, over-exposed photos.
  4. If your camera app allows it, switch off HDR and beauty filters.
Levels and method

Level 0, fresh or acceptable: ΔE00 below about 4. Level 1, slight to moderate browning: about 4 to 8. Level 2, severe browning: above about 8. The grade comes from a linear discriminant model on eight colour indices (L*, a*, b*, chroma, hue, browning index, whiteness index, yellowness index) measured after a local white-reference correction. On phones it had not seen during training, it graded 89 % of cubes and 90 % of photos correctly (balanced accuracy). Model trained on 324 cubes photographed with POCO C75, Oppo A3 and Realme C73 phones (dataset DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17087416).

Install on your phone

Android (Chrome): tap Install app below, or open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After the first visit the app works without internet.
